Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to mobile applications and other software published by PNN Studios ("we", "our", or "us").

We respect your privacy and are committed to being transparent about how our applications handle information. Unless otherwise stated within a specific application, our apps are designed to operate primarily on your device and do not collect or store personal information on PNN Studios servers.

Details that apply only to a particular app are listed under App-Specific Details below.

  • Account or connection information entered by you

Information We Collect

PNN Studios does not directly collect, transmit, or store personal information on our own servers unless otherwise disclosed within the applicable application.

Our applications may communicate with external services when required to provide functionality requested by you.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you are asked for consent before ads are personalised, and you can change your choice at any time from the app's settings.

App-Specific Details

Prinika – Family Meal Planner

Prinika has no account and no PNN Studios server. Your recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, stores, pantry items, reminders and settings are stored only on your device.

  • Recipe import: when you paste or share a link, or search websites you have added, the app downloads that page directly from the website, the same way a web browser does.
  • YouTube: to read recipe videos and search channels you add, the app uses YouTube API Services. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies.
  • Gemini (optional): if you add your own Google Gemini API key, the app sends the link of a recipe video to Google's Gemini service with your key so it can read the recipe. The key is stored only on your device and is not included in backups.
  • On-device features: reading text from photos of recipes, translating recipes, reading steps aloud and on-device AI tidying (Gemini Nano, on supported phones) run on your device. Photos are not uploaded.
  • Pictures: recipe pictures are loaded from the website or YouTube they came from.
  • Ads: the free version shows a limited number of Google AdMob ads, as described under Advertising. Ad privacy choices are in Settings when your region requires them.
  • Prinika Pro: bought through Google Play. Payment is handled by Google; the app only receives confirmation that you have Pro. Pro has no ads.
  • Backups: backup files are saved only where you choose (for example a folder on your phone). Android may also include app data in your own Google account backup if you have that turned on.
  • Permissions: notifications (only for features you turn on: planning reminder, scheduled backup, cooking timers, notes while watching a video), running after restart (so reminders and scheduled backups continue), and internet access. Taking a photo uses your phone's camera app; Prinika does not request camera or microphone permission.
