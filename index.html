Privacy policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Prinika is built so that your meal plans and recipes stay on your phone. There is no Prinika account and no Prinika server: we do not collect, store or sell your recipes, plans, grocery lists or personal details.

What stays on your phone

  • Recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, stores, pantry items, settings and reminders are stored only on your device.
  • Reading text from photos (cookbook pages, handwritten cards) and translating recipes happen on your phone with Google's on-device tools. The photos are not uploaded.
  • On supported phones, the on-device Gemini Nano model may tidy up an imported recipe. This also runs on the phone.

When the app connects to the internet

  • Importing a recipe: when you paste or share a link, or search your favourite websites, Prinika downloads that web page directly from the website, the same way a browser would.
  • YouTube: to read recipe videos and search channels you add, Prinika uses the YouTube Data API. By using these features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service; see also the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Gemini (optional): if you add your own Google Gemini API key, Prinika sends the link of a recipe video to Google's Gemini service using your key so it can read the recipe. Your key is stored only on your phone and is not included in backups. Google's terms for your key apply.
  • Pictures: recipe pictures are loaded from the websites or YouTube they came from.

Ads

The free version shows a limited number of ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising ID and information about your device and app use to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings. Learn more in how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Prinika Pro subscribers see no ads.

Purchases

Prinika Pro is bought through Google Play. Payment is handled entirely by Google; Prinika only receives confirmation that you have Pro.

Backups

Backups are files that you choose to save to a folder on your phone or to a storage app of your choice. Android may also include the app's data in your own Google account backup if you have that turned on. We never see these files.

Permissions

  • Notifications: only for features you turn on (planning reminder, scheduled backup, cooking timers, notes while watching a video).
  • Run at startup: so your reminder and scheduled backup still happen after the phone restarts.
  • Internet: for importing recipes, YouTube, pictures, optional Gemini and ads, as described above.

Children

Prinika is a meal-planning app for adults and families and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. For any privacy question, email pnnstudiosapp@gmail.com.
